## PART 1 — the one-shot master prompt (paste whole, once)

> Create ONE continuous 180-second teaser trailer video. Dark premium tech
> aesthetic, photorealistic 3D architectural visualization, cinematic lighting,
> smooth slow camera throughout, shallow depth of field. One consistent
> modern single-story villa and one consistent dark-blue and amber palette
> across every act. 720p, 16:9.
>
> ACT 1, 0:00-0:20, THE HOOK: Top-down close-up of an architectural floor-plan
> blueprint on a dark desk. Thin blue light traces the drawn walls until the
> plan glows. A bright scan line sweeps across it, and wherever it passes,
> flat walls extrude upward into clean white 3D walls. Slow push-in.
>
> ACT 2, 0:20-0:50, THE HOUSE AWAKENS: Slow cinematic orbit around the finished
> 3D villa at dusk. The dark roof lifts off and hovers. Warm lamps glow inside
> every room. Two glass doors swing open by themselves. Camera keeps one
> smooth orbital move the entire act.
>
> ACT 3, 0:50-1:15, LIGHT AND TIME: Time-lapse over the villa. Long morning
> shadows sweep across the rooms, the sky warms golden, then night falls and
> every window ignites warm from within. One slow wide arc, never stopping.
>
> ACT 4, 1:15-1:45, WALKTHROUGH: Smooth first-person glide through an open
> doorway into a cozy living room with a flickering fireplace. Drift left
> past a dining set toward tall glass windows. Calm, floating, no sudden moves.
>
> ACT 5, 1:45-2:30, REMODEL EVERYTHING: Montage, still slow: a wall slides to
> a new position; a sofa fades in, then a bed, then a dining set; glowing
> detection boxes flicker over a floor plan; a smartphone on a desk projects
> the tiny house as a hologram. Gentle transitions only.
>
> ACT 6, 2:30-3:00, FINALE: Slow majestic orbit of the complete glowing villa
> at night, gradually pulling back. Last 5 seconds: fade to black.
>
> STRICT RULES: same house design in every act; same palette throughout; no
> humans, no hands, no animals; no readable text, words, numbers or logos
> anywhere; no flicker, no morphing or melting geometry; nothing fast or shaky.

If one act renders badly, accept the video anyway — cut that act in editing
and stretch the others. Do not burn a regeneration.
