# Demo narration script (word-for-word, ~2:25)

Read at a calm demo pace over the recording. Bracketed cues are ACTIONS, not spoken.
Assumes: villa sample loaded → Play tour → Walk it live. Total video target under 3:00.

---

**[0:00 — source floor-plan image fullscreen]**
"Every renovation starts here: a flat drawing nobody can truly read. Homeowners guess,
contractors re-measure, and mistakes surface when fixes cost ten times more."

**[0:15 — click villa sample, extraction lands, scroll the 2D comparison]**
"BlueprintTo3D ends the guessing. Muse Spark reads the plan — every wall, door, window
and room — into exact 3D data. Then a second AI pass audits the work, and SAM
independently re-detects every symbol. Disagreements get flagged, never hidden."

**[0:45 — click Play tour; establishing shot]**
"And here's the house — orbit it, strip the roof, and fly room to room."

**[1:00 — tour room beats, doors swinging open]**
"Every door and window physically opens. Five bedrooms, two baths, terrace — all
measured, all clickable."

**[1:20 — sun sweep beat]**
"Full sun control from six AM to eight PM — watch the shadows sweep before you commit
to a single brick."

**[1:30 — night beat, window glow]**
"And after dark, every room glows exactly where your lamps will be."

**[1:40 — click Walk it live; walk forward, open a door with E]**
"But renders lie — so walk it. Full first person: collision, jumping, footsteps."

**[2:00 — click a door, panel opens, click Recommend, Apply]**
"Click anything to change it. Muse tells you what belongs in that exact opening —
and applies it live."

**[2:15 — quick Build montage: drag a wall, undo; flash Verify tab + Export tab]**
"Remodel anything with undo, verify with SAM, export to GLB, DXF and CSV for real tools."

**[2:25 — end card / orbit wide, hold 3 seconds]**
"BlueprintTo3D. From paper plan to house in sixty seconds."

---

## Delivery notes
- Record narration AFTER the screen capture, in one quiet take, then lay it under the video.
- Leave 1 second of breathing room at each [bracket] so cuts land cleanly.
- If any live call lags, keep talking over it ("while Muse reads this…") — never dead air.
- No API keys on screen at any point. Blur the header key badge if unsure.
